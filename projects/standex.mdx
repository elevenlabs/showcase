## The Problem

Daily standups are essential for team alignment, but they often suffer from:

- **Lost context** — Action items mentioned in standup get forgotten
- **Manual overhead** — Someone has to update the task board after every meeting
- **Time wasted** — 15+ minutes per standup just to share updates
- **Async challenges** — Distributed teams struggle with timezone differences

## The Solution

**Standex** automatically transforms your standup messages into structured insights and actionable tasks. Just share what you're working on — the AI handles the rest.

## Features

- **Smart Standups** — Share your update naturally (text or voice), and AI automatically structures it into Yesterday, Today, and Blockers
- **AI Task Extraction** — The AI analyzes your standup and creates new tasks, moves completed work to done, assigns priorities, and sets due dates automatically
- **Visual Kanban Board** — Drag-and-drop task management with priority badges, due date tracking, and team assignments
- **Voice Input** — Record your standup hands-free with ElevenLabs transcription capturing every detail
- **Team Collaboration** — Real-time presence indicators, invite links for easy onboarding, and shared visibility into team progress
- **Daily Summaries** — ElevenLabs audio summaries keep everyone aligned on team progress

## How It Works

1. **Share your update** — Type or speak what you did
2. **AI analyzes** — Parses message and identifies tasks
3. **Board updates** — Tasks created, completed work marked done
