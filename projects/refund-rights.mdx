# RefundRights

## Overview

RefundRights is an intelligent AI agent designed to help travelers hassle-free secure refunds for flights, hotels, and travel bookings. Dealing with customer service or navigating complex cancellation policies can be exhausting; RefundRights automates this entire process by comparing platform policies with local consumer protection laws, drafting professional appeal letters, and driving the dispute process forward to significantly increase the success rate.

By integrating **ElevenLabs Speech-to-Text (STT)** and **Text-to-Speech (TTS)** features, RefundRights simplifies user inputs and delivers enhanced, seamless audio outputs.
## Key Features

- Feature 1: - **Automated Refund Appeals**: Analyzes travel policies and generates legally-grounded refund requests in seconds.
- Feature 2: - **AI Voice Agent Hotline Navigation**: *(Roadmap)* Built-in capabilities to call customer service lines and navigate IVR menus automatically.
- Feature 3: - **Dynamic Emotion & Tone Control**: *(Roadmap)* Adapting voice performance dynamically based on the interaction to maintain a firm yet polite stance.
- Feature 4:- **Multilingual Support**: Breaks down language barriers with multi-language capabilities, helping users dispute international travel issues effortlessly.

## How It Works

RefundRights utilizes a multi-agent architecture. When a user submits a travel dispute, our LLM agent analyzes the case, checks regional consumer protection laws, and formulates a strategic action plan.

*(Under Development)* For voice-based disputes, the system will connect to a VoIP pipeline. The LLM's text responses will be streamed directly into the **ElevenLabs Speech Synthesis API** using low-latency WebSockets, ensuring real-time, fluid conversations with customer service operators. We also plan to leverage ElevenLabs' Voice Cloning to allow users to optionally use authorized, professional voice profiles for their phone appeals.


## Technologies Used

- **ElevenLabs API**:
  - Speech-to-Text (STT) for effortless voice input.
  - Text-to-Speech (TTS) via WebSocket for real-time, low-latency audio outputs.
- **AI & Development Platforms**: Lovable.dev, Gemini 3.1
- **Payment Processing**: Stripe
