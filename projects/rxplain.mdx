# RxPlain

## Overview

RxPlain is an educational accessibility tool that translates dense medication labels, dosage limits, and food interactions into plain, readable language and interactive visual maps for patients facing health literacy, vision, or language barriers. To ensure these vital summaries are accessible to everyone, we integrated ElevenLabs technology to deliver clear, human-like voice read-alouds for our Spanish interface, ensuring non-English speakers can hear and understand their prescriptions with natural clarity.   
## Key Features

Plain-Language Dose & Food Interaction Warnings: Translates complex openFDA label data and food interactions into simple, actionable summaries without jargon.


Multimodal Bottle Scanning & Safety Guardrails: Scans prescription bottles via camera or phone QR capture, parses directions using Grok vision, and strictly checks doses against official FDA daily maximums before confirming.


Accessible Dual-Language Read-Alouds: Built-in voice read-aloud functionality providing English and Spanish audio for all medication explanations, community experiences, and label summaries.



## How It Works

RxPlain is built with Next.js (App Router) and TypeScript, deployed on the DigitalOcean App Platform. All third-party vendor calls are executed strictly inside `/api/` route handlers to keep credentials off the client. The core data layer utilizes an adapter pattern (`lib/db`), querying Snowflake when configured or falling back to structured JSON seed datasets (HRSA/CMS clinics, openFDA label sections, dose limits, and glossary mappings).

For dose checking, the system retrieves official FDA administration and overdosage text, verifies daily totals against structured limits, and passes the text to Grok to generate a plain-language restatement. Strict guardrails enforce numeric grounding and fail closed whenever a dose cannot be verified. For audio accessibility, `lib/tts/` handles dynamic routing based on language preference: English requests route to xAI Grok Voice, while Spanish text passes through a translation layer that protects numbers and units before generating high-fidelity audio via ElevenLabs.

## Technologies Used

-ElevenLabs API: Text-to-Speech (TTS) for natural Spanish voice synthesis


-xAI Grok API: Grok Vision for bottle OCR scanning, Grok Chat for plain-text rewrites/summaries, and Grok Voice for English TTS


-openFDA API & RxNorm: Official drug label evidence lookup and deterministic drug name normalization


-Snowflake Database: Cloud data warehouse for structured interaction querying, drug caches, and session storage


-Frameworks & Libraries: Next.js (App Router), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Cytoscape.js


-Infrastructure: DigitalOcean App Platform
